# Gatherd — Vision

> **Gatherd exists to help people create memorable moments together. Games are the medium, not the
> product.**

## Purpose

Most "party game" apps optimize for the game. Gatherd optimizes for the **people around it** — the
laughter, the inside jokes, the "remember when," the reason a group keeps coming back. A game is the
excuse to gather; the moment it creates is the point.

This single idea is the tie-breaker for every product and engineering decision. When two designs are
equally clean, we pick the one that produces better shared moments.

## Mission

Build the home for playing together — a platform that hosts a growing library of games (classic and
original) on one engine, wraps them in a social layer that remembers what matters, and feels
unmistakably like Gatherd no matter which game you open.

## Who it's for

- **Friend groups** who already hang out (in person or over a call) and want something to do.
- **New connections** who meet through public rooms and become regulars.
- **Hosts** — the one person who organizes game night — for whom Gatherd should feel like a superpower.

## The experience we want

1. **Effortless to gather.** A room is seconds away. A code or a link is all it takes to join — no
   install wall, no signup wall. Friction is the enemy of spontaneity.
2. **Instantly familiar.** Every game lives inside the same Gatherd shell, so the second game is as
   easy as the first. You learn Gatherd once.
3. **Alive between sessions.** Progression, friends, memories, and seasonal moments mean Gatherd is
   warm even when you're not mid-game. It remembers your group.
4. **Worth returning to.** The reward isn't just points — it's "that was the funniest round," a
   one-year-of-playing-together memory, a new game dropping for the holidays.

## Core principles

1. **Moments over metrics.** Stats are a means; memories are the end. (See `docs/memories.md`.)
2. **The group is the unit.** Design for the table, not the solo player. Shared state, shared joy,
   shared history.
3. **One Gatherd, many games.** Games vary in rules and art; the platform's feel never does.
   (See `docs/design-language.md`.)
4. **Lower the floor, raise the ceiling.** Trivial to start a game; deep enough to keep mastering and
   collecting.
5. **Server-authoritative, player-trusting.** The server is the source of truth so play is fair even
   among strangers; players are trusted with frictionless guest access.
6. **Modular by default.** A new game or platform feature is a new module/consumer, never a rewrite.
7. **Lean now, ready for scale.** Design for where we're going; build only what this phase needs.
8. **Originals build the brand.** We host the classics and create first-party games that become part
   of Gatherd's identity. (See `docs/first-party-games.md`.)

## What success looks like

A group opens Gatherd on a Friday out of habit, plays two games in fifteen minutes, laughs at a
callback to last week, and a "you've played together for a year" memory surfaces — and none of them
think about the app at all. They think about each other.
